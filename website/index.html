A tiny step counter that does one thing: count your steps toward a hundred. Milestones at 10, 20, 50. No sign-up, no sync, no floor of the month. Just the number, your phone's sensor, and a bar that fills up.

01 A bar with a brass ring

One hundred steps, full stop. The bar fills as you go, and the markers at 10 · 20 · 50 light up when you hit them. Count past a hundred if you like — the bar just stays honest.

02 No background anything

Counting happens only while the app is open. Close it, and it stops. No health sync, no location, no server, not even a login. It can't chase you around the internet.

03 Built to be tested

Sitting at a desk? Tap the +1 Step button. Working in the office? The hardware step sensor reads real motion. Reset re-baselines the sensor, so the count always starts fresh.
